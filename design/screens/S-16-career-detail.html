Electrical technician
RealisticInvestigativeHands-on

Installs and repairs wiring, solar systems and machines in homes, shops and factories.

How to get there in Pakistan

DAE Electrical (3 years) → job, or B.Tech / BS Engineering Technology

Short course: TEVTA or NAVTTC certificate (3–12 months)

What the work is like

Practical, safety-critical, often on site. Growing demand with solar.
