# Naya Shehar — Player & Teacher Guide

## For players

**The story.** A flood damaged the small town of Naya Shehar. The town has a grant to rebuild,
and you have joined Baji Saima's Youth Development Office as a volunteer.

**How a week works**

1. **Season card.** See what the town needs this season and which places have opened.
2. **Meet the team.** Short story scenes. Tap *Next*, or the speaker to hear them read aloud.
3. **Plan your week.** Pick the 3 jobs you most want to do and put your favourite first.
   Use ↑ and ↓ to change the order.
4. **Choose how to help.** Again and again you will pick one of two jobs. There is no right or
   wrong answer — choose what you would really enjoy.
5. **Do the job.** Every job is a small game: fix pipes, find clues, paint a mural, help
   neighbours, gather a team or sort records.
6. **How was it?** You can tap *Meh*, *Okay* or *Loved it* — or just *Skip*.
7. **Challenges.** Puzzles about shapes, patterns, numbers, clues, planning and words.
   There is no timer. If you get one wrong you can try again.
8. **Build!** Watch the season's big building rise, stage by stage.
9. **A letter.** Open a letter from yourself, five years in the future.
10. **Come back next week.** The next season opens 7 days later.

**The town map.** Drag to look around. Tap a district button to jump there, or a building to see
it up close. Use grant money (earned by doing activities and challenges) to fund more building work.
Locked areas have a padlock pin and open in later weeks.

**The town meters** — Homes, Happiness and Balance — show how your town is doing. They are just
for fun.

**Pause** is the orange button at the top left on every game screen. Your game is saved all
the time, so you can close the app whenever you like and continue later.

**At the end** you will see the whole town, a summary of your year, your final letter, and some
study paths that might suit you, with reasons. These are ideas to talk about with your family and
teachers — not a test result.

## For teachers and parents

- **Time:** prologue ≈ 10 min; each of the four weeks ≈ 15–20 min; one week per real week.
- **Safety:** no ads, purchases, chat, links, sign-in or typing. Nicknames come from a fixed list.
  The only personal information asked is class and Matric group, at the very end.
- **Offline:** works with no internet. Everything is stored on the phone.
- **Accessibility:** read-aloud on all text, larger text, reduced motion, dark theme and an
  *accessible task mode*. In accessible task mode students play normally, but those weeks are
  marked *not measured* and are left out of the career suggestions.
- **Interpreting results:** the suggestions come from an early, unvalidated scoring model and
  draft admission rules. Use them to start a conversation, never to decide for a student. Always
  check admission requirements with the relevant board or college.
- **Shared phones:** Settings › *Start over* wipes the game on that phone. There is one game per phone.
