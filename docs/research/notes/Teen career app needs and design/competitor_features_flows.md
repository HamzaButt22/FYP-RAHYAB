# Competitor Feature Sets, User Flows and Engagement Design (Career Guidance for Secondary Students)

Research date: 2026-10-29. This builds on `Career guidance games app Pakistan/competitors.md` (pricing and basics are not repeated unless needed). About 20 tool calls were used. Most evidence is **vendor or district documentation** (marked [V]). Independent evidence (student newspapers, review sites, competitor blogs) is marked [I]. Competitor-authored review blogs (futuremeanswered, mypassion.ai, JobCannon) have a commercial interest and are marked [I-competitor].

## Feature list per product (feature-by-competitor matrix)

### Takeaway
US school platforms (Xello, Naviance, Kuder, Pathful) are **multi-year planning systems**: assessment, career library, saving, goals, course planner, college search, portfolio, read-only parent view and educator reports. Indian players (Mindler, iDreamCareer, Univariety) are **assessment, long PDF report and paid counsellor** funnels with a career library. Pakistani players are **test plus counsellor booking** websites with thin product surfaces. Of all the products reviewed, only YouScience uses game-based assessment.

None of them combines all four of the following: game-based assessment, stream-level output, a parent-facing report and mobile-first engagement.

### Inferences
- **Table stakes in every mature product:** assessment, results, career library and saved items. Planning features (goals, course planner) come next.
- **Rarer features:** portfolio, college applications and scholarships appear mainly in US platforms because of their school-workflow mandates. They are low priority for a Pakistani Grade 9–12 MVP.
- **Parent access is weak everywhere:**
  - Xello is read-only and gated by the school.
  - Kuder links parents through an access code from the student account.
  - Mindler involves parents only in paid counselling.
  - No product found offers a parent-tailored explanation report.
- **Gamification is almost absent outside the assessment.** Xello uses avatars and customisation. YouScience uses games only to assess. No streaks, XP or badges were found in any career product. That would be differentiating for Rahyaab, but it is also unvalidated in this category.

### Gaps
- Not found:
  - Official YouScience results-page screenshots or description.
  - CareerGuide (India) features beyond pricing.
  - Merafuture and ilmkidunya product details: Merafuture returned HTTP 500 in earlier research, and ilmkidunya returned no career tool in search.
  - Univariety's own feature pages.
- Language support and offline mode are unverified for most products.
- The matrix cells marked "?" mean not found, not absent.

## Typical user journeys (onboarding → assessment → results → exploration → planning → sharing → follow-up)

### Takeaway
There are two dominant journey archetypes:
- **(A) School-orchestrated, longitudinal:** used by Xello, Naviance, Kuder and Pathful.
- **(B) Consumer funnel:** used by Mindler, CareerCoachs, CareerExplorer, 16Personalities and YouScience individual.

Rahyaab is B2C-first in Pakistan, so it fits archetype B, but it should borrow two features from A: saved items and parent linking.

### Cited Findings
**Archetype A: school platform (Xello as the reference)**
1. **Onboarding:** The school provisions the account through SSO and SIS rostering. There is no individual sign-up. — [Xello Features](https://xello.world/en/features-gallery/); [futuremeanswered Xello review](https://www.futuremeanswered.com/xello-review)
2. **Assessment:** Interest matchmaker → Holland follow-up → Skills Lab → learning styles. Each assessment can be retaken. — [Dubuque CSD](https://www.dbqschools.org/xello/); [Amador Valley Today](https://www.amadorvalleytoday.org/14684/news/counselors-roll-out-the-new-xello-program)
3. **Results:** Career matches. — [Xello Features](https://xello.world/en/features-gallery/)
4. **Exploration:** Career profiles, interviews, clusters and the personalised feed. — [Xello Features](https://xello.world/en/features-gallery/)
5. **Saving:** Students save careers, schools and clusters. Educator "Student intentions" reports count how many students have saved at least one of each. — [Xello Help: Student intentions](https://help.xello.world/en-US/Content/Knowledge-Base/Xello-6-12/Reports/Report_Student-Intentions.htm)
6. **Planning:** Course planner and goals. — [Xello Features](https://xello.world/en/features-gallery/)
7. **Sharing:** Portfolio link and read-only parent portal. — [Xello Features](https://xello.world/en/features-gallery/)
8. **Follow-up:** Lessons assigned per grade, educator messaging, and engagement reports of logins by day, month and student, per school year starting August 1. — [Xello Help: Student engagement](https://help.xello.world/en-CA/Content/Knowledge-Base/KB/Report_Student-Engagement.htm)
- **Kuder variant:** a Career Planning Timeline gives the sequence of self-knowledge → education plan → post-secondary preparation, and parents attach through a code from the student account. — [Kuder Help](https://help.kuder.com/en/articles/8173342-get-to-know-navigator); [Nebraska quick guide](https://nebraska.kuder.com/Resources/pdf/K64~TkUyLVF1aWNrLUd1aWRlfDcyfDB8ZW4)

**Archetype B: consumer funnel (Mindler as the reference)**
1. **Free preview:** Part 1 of the assessment only.
2. **Paywall:** A payment unlocks the full assessment and the 25- or 34-page report.
3. **Results:** Top 2 streams or top 5 careers.
4. **Counselling upsell:** Includes pre-counselling activities, a 60-minute session that parents join, then career shortlisting (best fit plus a backup), and course and college selection.
5. **Follow-up:** One year of query support. The top tier adds goal tracking with a mentor.
- [Mindler pricing](https://www.mindler.com/pricing)

**Other consumer flows**
- **CareerCoachs:** free test → basic report → paid detailed report plus counsellor video session. — [CareerCoachs](https://careercoachs.com/online-career-counselling/pakistan)
- **CareerExplorer:** questions → "what makes you unique" (archetype) → explore school and work options → upgrade. — [CareerExplorer](https://www.careerexplorer.com/)
- **16Personalities:** test → type with trait percentages, Role and Strategy → free profile → paid premium report. — [Scribd copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/462956257/Test); [Pro Suite](https://www.16personalities.com/premium/pro-suite)

### Inferences
- **Recommended Rahyaab journey:**
  1. Light onboarding: grade, board, city, language.
  2. Game-based assessment split into 3–5 short sessions, following YouScience's practice of letting students split games across sittings.
  3. Results: top stream plus a backup stream with an explanation.
  4. Explore careers under that stream.
  5. Save and compare.
  6. Share a parent report by WhatsApp link or PDF.
  7. Follow-up: an optional counsellor booking and a reassessment prompt before the Grade 9 and Grade 11 decisions.
- Mindler's "best fit plus a backup" shortlist maps neatly onto the Pakistani Pre-Med vs Pre-Eng/ICS decision. A backup stream is important given MDCAT seat odds (see the earlier notes).

### Gaps
- No vendor published step-by-step onboarding screen counts or funnel conversion data, apart from CareerExplorer's testimonial claim that 70%+ who start the test complete it.

## Typical session and usage durations; engagement metrics

### Takeaway
Assessment lengths range from 10 minutes to 2.5 hours:

| Assessment | Length |
|---|---|
| O*NET IP | 10–20 min |
| CareerExplorer | ~20 min |
| CareerCoachs | ~30 min |
| YouScience | 60–90 min, splittable |
| Mindler | ~2.5 h |

No vendor publishes return frequency or retention. School platforms measure logins internally but do not publish benchmarks.

### Cited Findings
- **YouScience:** 11 games, about 90 minutes in total, can be taken over multiple sittings. — [JEL](https://www.jelcc.com/careerdiscovery); [futuremeanswered](https://www.futuremeanswered.com/youscience-review)
- **O*NET IP:** 10–20 minutes. — [O*NET Center](https://www.onetcenter.org/IP.html)
- **CareerExplorer:** 70%+ of starters complete the test right away (vendor testimonial). — [CareerExplorer](https://www.careerexplorer.com/)
- **Xello:** tracks logins by day, month and student, but no aggregate figures are published. — [Xello Help](https://help.xello.world/en-CA/Content/Knowledge-Base/KB/Report_Student-Engagement.htm)
- **Pathful:** job-shadow videos are 3–5 minutes, a snackable unit. — [Pathful blog](https://www.pathful.com/blog/the-power-of-pathfuls-virtual-job-shadowing-videos)
- **Naviance StrengthsExplorer:** about 78 questions, single attempt (third-party, via search summary). — [Naviance features PDF](https://1.cdn.edl.io/u6xjl10qDRumBO8G9Dbzam2lpHHzg20rIwYnaXH0pvq9Il2D.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Engagement is periodic, not daily.** In US platforms it is driven by educator-assigned lessons through the school year, not organic daily use. A Pakistani B2C app without school mandates should expect episodic use around decision points (after Grade 8 or 9 results, and Matric results). Retention features should target those moments rather than daily streaks.
- **Session length:** a total game time of about 30–45 minutes split into sessions of 10 minutes or less would be shorter than every questionnaire battery except O*NET's.

### Gaps
- No published DAU/MAU, return frequency or average session time was found for any competitor.
- No app-store engagement data was found for the Pakistani or Indian career apps.

## Most valued / most criticized features (reviews, case studies)

### Takeaway
The independent evidence is thin, anecdotal and mostly US student newspapers and review sites:
- **Valued:** retakes, career browsing, clean UI, and measuring ability rather than self-report.
- **Criticised:** clunky interfaces, binary or mismatched recommendations, inaccurate information, no action plan after the assessment, and a college-only bias.

Design responses for Rahyaab:

## Published design patterns of results pages (fit %, top-N, explanations)

### Takeaway
Results pages follow five recurring patterns:
1. A **label or archetype** (16Personalities type, CareerExplorer archetype).
2. **Trait or dimension percentages** (16Personalities).
3. **Top-N recommendations**: Mindler gives the top 2 streams and top 5 careers; Naviance the top 3 of 10 themes; Mindler's shortlist is a best fit plus a backup.
4. **Compatibility or fit scores per career** (CareerCoachs).
5. **Long PDF reports**: YouScience about 40 pages, Mindler 25 or 34 pages.

A free summary page with a paid deep report is the norm.

### Cited Findings
- **16Personalities:** type + trait % (for example "Introverted 65%") + Role + Strategy, then a premium upsell. — [Scribd copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/462956257/Test); [Roles Defined](https://16personalities.com/articles/roles-defined)
- **CareerExplorer:** career matches + personality archetype, with "more along the way". — [CareerExplorer](https://www.careerexplorer.com/)
- **Mindler:**
  - Top 2 streams with a 25-page report.
  - Top 5 careers with a 34-page report.
  - Counselling then narrows to a best fit plus a backup.
  - [Mindler pricing](https://www.mindler.com/pricing)
- **Naviance StrengthsExplorer:** top 3 of 10 themes. — [Naviance features PDF](https://1.cdn.edl.io/u6xjl10qDRumBO8G9Dbzam2lpHHzg20rIwYnaXH0pvq9Il2D.pdf)
- **CareerCoachs:** compatibility scores across 300+ careers. — [CareerCoachs](https://careercoachs.com/online-career-counselling/pakistan)
- **YouScience:** aptitude profile and a report of about 40 pages. — [Brightpath Discovery](https://www.youscience.com/education/brightpath/discovery/)
- **O*NET:** RIASEC interest scores linked to occupation lists. — [O*NET Center](https://www.onetcenter.org/IP.html)
- **Xello:** results enter the student profile and drive a personalised feed and lessons ("Insightful Student Profiles" show matches to educators). — [Xello Features](https://xello.world/en/features-gallery/)

### Inferences: MVP vs later for Rahyaab
**MVP**
1. Game-based assessment in short sessions, with save and resume.
2. A results page showing:
   - the top stream plus a backup, each with a fit indicator;
   - a strengths "archetype" label;
   - 2–3 plain-language reasons per recommendation;
   - next steps.
3. Stream → career library: Pakistan-specific careers, with entry tests (MDCAT/ECAT/NTS) for each.
4. Save or favourite careers.
5. A parent share in Urdu/English via WhatsApp link or a short PDF.
6. Reassessment.
7. Urdu and English.

**Later**
- Counsellor booking (a paid upsell, following CareerCoachs and Mindler).
- University and program search.
- Scholarships.
- Short career videos (the Pathful-style 3–5 minute format).
- Goal setting and subject planner.
- Portfolio.
- An educator/school dashboard (B2B).
- AI chat assistant.
- Alumni or mentor matching (the Univariety model).
- Notifications tied to decision dates (Matric results, admissions windows).

**Avoid**
- A long PDF as the primary output.
- Daily-streak gamification without evidence; engagement in this category is episodic.

### Gaps
- No official 16Personalities or YouScience description of current results-page layouts was found; the 16Personalities pattern comes from an unofficial copy.
- No A/B or usability research on fit-percentage versus ranked-list displays in career tools was found.
