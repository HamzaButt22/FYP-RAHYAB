# Stakeholder Requirements for Rahyaab: Parents, Counsellors/Teachers, School Administrators, Government/NGO Channels (Pakistan-first)

Scope note: Earlier reports in /reports already cover Hassan et al. 2022 (parental β=.50), Taj 2025, Asghar & Javed 2018, Sadiq 2024, Keshf & Khanum 2022, Saya et al. 2025 (CDSE, n=1,445), PNAS 2017 parent intervention, and Carlana 2019. They are not repeated here except where needed. Research budget was ~15 tool calls; many requested specifics (PKR willingness to pay, Urdu PDF preferences, Beaconhouse/City School procurement rules, PEIMA requirements) had no public sources and are listed under Gaps.

## Parents: information needs, channels, trust/consent, willingness to pay

### Takeaway
Pakistani parents are the main decision-makers on stream choice, and for daughters their decisions are driven by safety, mobility and "respectability" (teaching, medicine). Rahyaab therefore has to give parents a report they can share, covering prospects, cost, entry tests and options for girls, and must get parental consent. Pakistan's data protection bill was still not law in 2026, but its draft bans behavioural monitoring of children and requires parental consent. No Pakistani public data exists on how much parents would pay for an app; the only price anchor found is a PKR 4,000 counselling session.

### Cited Findings
- A peer-reviewed survey of 200 secondary students (government and private schools) found parental influence affected career choice and gender stereotyping more than media did. Parent, media and stereotype factors together explained R² = 0.694 — [Frontiers in Psychology 2022 (PMC)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9274975/)
- An Eduvision survey of Islamabad college students reported that many still prefer careers their parents want; relatives and family friends were also sources of influence. This is a commercial survey and lower quality — [Eduvision](https://www.eduvision.edu.pk/edu_news/are-pakistani-students-making-the-right-career-choices-news-4230)
- Girls (safety and respectability): in a 2025 study of women's work in Pakistan, participants gravitated toward roles "deemed culturally acceptable and safe", such as teaching in girls' schools (40%) or healthcare in women's clinics (25%) — [arXiv 2508.16664](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.16664)
- A UK government (DFID) landscaping report: girls' mobility is restricted for safety reasons in urban areas and to protect family honour in rural areas — [Pakistan Girls Landscaping Report 2017](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/58f9cce240f0b606e70001af/Pakistan_Girls_Landscaping_Report_Apr2017.pdf)
- Dawn ("doctor brides") reports that parents, mainly fathers, encouraged daughters into medicine as a "respectable" and "noble" field and as a "safety net" if a marriage failed — [Dawn 2019](https://www.dawn.com/news/1460104). This conflicts with a Karachi JPMA pilot study (n=141), where 71.6% of women doctors said the choice was their own and only 12.8% said parents compelled them — [JPMA](https://archive.jpma.org.pk/article-details/8567)
- A UCD analysis notes parents' security concerns because most universities are co-educational and require travel away from home — [UCD Research Repository](https://researchrepository.ucd.ie/entities/publication/740309be-520b-4072-b15b-cb665cdacae8)
- A study of female teachers in Hazara Division found mobility restrictions, family obligations and social expectations steer women toward teaching and academia — [GCWUS ORJSS](https://ojs-orjss.gcwus.edu.pk/journal/article/download/45/40/75)
- A Dawn reader letter argues that parents, not only students, need career counselling — [Dawn 2020](https://www.dawn.com/news/1597563)
- Channels: a UMT paper reports an "extreme lack of communication and consultancy" between Pakistani parents and teachers and proposes an Android parent-teacher portal — [UMT eScholar, PTAP](https://escholar.umt.edu.pk/handle/123456789/18486). A Pakistani parent in the UAE said WhatsApp removes the need to keep visiting the school — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/education/whatsapp-fb-groups-help-reduce-back-to-school-stress-1.1879468); a survey of UAE parents found WhatsApp is their most preferred tool for school communication — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/news/education/whatsapp-most-preferred-tool-for-uae-parents-survey) (Gulf evidence, not Pakistan). An Israeli study calls for a clear, uniform policy on teacher-parent WhatsApp use — [Open University of Israel, Chais 2024](https://www.openu.ac.il/Lists/MediaServer_Documents/innovation/chais/2024/b4_4.pdf)
- Data protection status: Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Bill (2023 draft) "has yet to be passed by both houses of the Parliament", and the National Commission for Personal Data Protection is not operating. The draft requires age verification, consent from "a parent or legally authorised guardian", and states that "tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children are prohibited" — [Chambers Data Protection & Privacy 2026: Pakistan](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/pakistan); see also [ProPakistani 2023 on child data focus](https://propakistani.pk/2023/05/24/govt-to-specially-focus-on-protecting-childrens-info-under-data-protection-bill-2023/) and [MoITT draft text](https://moitt.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/Final%20Draft%20Personal%20Data%20Protection%20Bill%20May%202023.pdf). Proposed fines are up to about USD 2 million — [ProPakistani 2024](https://propakistani.pk/2024/05/22/it-ministry-finalizes-data-protection-bill-with-fines-up-to-2-million/). PECA 2016 is the operative law on electronic data — [Chambers](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/pakistan)
- Price anchors: Eduvision charges PKR 4,000 per career counselling session (45 min online / 2 h in person), including aptitude/personality tests and a 6-page report — [Eduvision](https://www.eduvision.edu.pk/careers/index.php). Maqsad's consumer edtech price is about Rs 500–600/month for all subjects — [Profit/Pakistan Today](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2023/03/16/despite-global-downturn-ed-tech-sector-sees-hope-with-maqsads-2-8-million-round). In India, Mindler reportedly starts at ₹1,499+; this figure comes from a competitor's comparison page and is unverified — [CorpReady360](https://corpready.in/compare/why-not-mindler)

### Inferences
- The parent report should answer the questions families actually raise: job prospects and salary, total cost and duration of study, entry tests (MDCAT/ECAT and similar), whether study is possible locally or near home, and "respectable/safe" options for daughters. The draft bill's behavioural-monitoring ban matters because gameplay telemetry used for scoring could count as behavioural monitoring of children. Rahyaab should keep telemetry minimal and purpose-limited, explain it to parents, and get verifiable parental consent now, before the law is passed.
- WhatsApp-shareable output (an image or PDF in Urdu and English) is plausible as the main channel, but this rests on Gulf and general evidence plus Pakistan's low parent-teacher contact. It is not supported by Pakistani survey data.
- If consumers pay directly, a price well below PKR 4,000 per student (one session's cost), roughly the cost of a month of consumer edtech (PKR 500–1,000), is a reasonable hypothesis to test. It is not evidence.

### Gaps
- No Pakistani survey found on parents' willingness to pay for career guidance or apps, in PKR.
- No Pakistani data found on parents' preferred format (Urdu PDF, WhatsApp, PTM) for career information.
- No Pakistani study found on parents' trust in or concerns about apps collecting minors' data.
- I did not check whether provincial child-protection or education laws impose additional consent rules.

## Counsellors/teachers: workflows, dashboards, flags, reports, training

### Takeaway
Most Pakistani schools, and almost all public ones, have no counsellor, so in practice the "counsellor" will be a class teacher with little time. Global vendors (Xello, YouScience, Naviance, Mindler) converge on the same features: class/school dashboards, completion and engagement reports, a one-page student summary, alerts, exports, and teacher/parent workshops. Rahyaab's educator side should copy that minimal set.

### Cited Findings
- A Karachi study of high school students found very few schools offer career counselling sessions and "no concept of presence of career counsellors in any school of Pakistan" — [IJCMPH 2017](https://www.ijcmph.com/index.php/ijcmph/article/view/1435)
- APP: a shortage of trained career counsellors, especially in public schools; some private schools do better (one Karachi principal reports two qualified counsellors) — [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1004801)
- A Tribune letter claims about 60% of Pakistani schools are public and none of them are aware of career counselling (opinion; dates on the page are unreliable) — [Tribune letter](https://tribune.com.pk/letter/2253655/career-counselling); another letter calls for a nationally regulated framework starting at middle school — [Tribune letter](https://tribune.com.pk/letter/2257988/career-guidance-crisis)
- Global Shapers Islamabad: most students outside elite or private institutions lack career counselling; Islamabad has more than 1,380 schools — [Global Shapers CC4All](https://www.globalshapers.org/impact/career-counselling-for-all-cc4all/)
- Teacher workload: about 30% of government schools (vs 20% of private) have one teacher managing several grades (Gallup Pakistan, reported June 2026) — [ProPakistani 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/06/24/30-of-govt-schools-in-pakistan-have-one-teacher-managing-multiple-classrooms/)
- Xello: educator dashboards and reports track student progress. The Reports tool covers student logins, assessment completion and popular suggested careers, can be run for a whole school or a small group, and reports can be exported. Critical alerts and two-way messaging help keep students on track — [Xello Educator tools](https://xello.world/en/educator/); [Xello Report tips](https://help.xello.world/en-US/Content/Knowledge-Base/KB/Report_Tips-Tricks.htm); [Xello reports on student work](https://help.xello.world/en-gb/Content/Knowledge-Base/Xello-6-12/Reports/Report_About-Me-Profile.htm). A reseller listing describes dashboards for students, classes and districts with engagement, lesson-completion and compliance reports — [SoftwareOne](https://platform.softwareone.com/product/educator-tools/PCP-0756-2271)
- YouScience Brightpath (2025 update): a new centralized counselor dashboard, engagement metrics, a student details dashboard, flexibility for in-class use, a Spanish translation option, and a "Counselor Summary Report" that exports a single-page summary of each student's aptitude and career results — [YouScience blog 2025](https://www.youscience.com/resources/blog/whats-new-in-brightpath-back-to-school-2025-product-updates); [2025 webinar slides](https://resources.youscience.com/rs/806-BFU-539/images/2025_BackToSchoolWebinar_Slides.pdf); [YouScience for counselors](https://www.youscience.com/counselors/)
- Naviance (PowerSchool, 2025–26): a redesigned dashboard, simpler workflows and "real-time visibility into student progress" to help counselors manage caseloads — [Silicon UK press release](https://www.silicon.co.uk/press-release/powerschool-unveils-major-enhancements-to-naviance-for-the-2025-2026-school-year); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250722073696/en). No Naviance "flag" feature was confirmed.
- Mindler (India) school model: a dedicated school dashboard with analytics and real-time tracking of student profiles, alumni tracking, teacher and parent workshops, and certification of principals and counsellors. The source deck is from 2021 and may be outdated — [Scribd partner deck](https://www.scribd.com/document/510572241/Partner-PPT-VC15); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/weekender/this-platform-provides-a-one-stop-solution-to-career-guidance-mindler); student and parent orientation sessions run at partner schools — [Jaipuria](https://sajk.jaipuria.edu.in/mindler-student-parent-orientation-session/)
- South Asia comparison (Indian Punjab, not Pakistan): the state is training more than 5,000 government teachers as "career mentors" with IIT Madras Pravartak (free online training in basic counselling and one-to-one guidance), aimed at rural students without counsellors — [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/india/punjab-partners-with-iit-madras-to-train-5000-teachers-as-career-mentors-1503522099.html); it is also piloting 25 AI career-guidance labs with aptitude assessment and personalised reports — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/punjab-government-launches-ai-driven-career-guidance-labs-public-pseb-schools-25-students-aptitude-assessment-personalised-reports/amp); and running psychometric tests for about 93,819 Class 10 girls — [Sentinel Assam](https://www.sentinelassam.com/more-news/national-news/punjab-to-conduct-psychometric-tests-for-class-10-girl-students)

### Inferences
- Minimum educator feature set: (1) class roster with completion status; (2) class-level distribution of recommended streams; (3) automatic flags, e.g., not started or incomplete, a large mismatch between interest and aptitude, a chosen stream that differs from the recommendation, or rushed or low-effort play; (4) a one-page printable student summary for parent-teacher meetings; (5) short notes per student; (6) Excel/CSV export. The Indian Punjab "teacher as career mentor" model suggests Rahyaab should include a short (1–2 hour) teacher training module rather than assume trained counsellors exist.
- Given multigrade teaching and heavy workloads, any teacher task should take minutes per class, not per student.

### Gaps
- No Pakistani study found that observes how teachers or counsellors actually run stream-selection sessions, how long they spend, or what reports they want.
- No public documentation found of counselling practice inside Beaconhouse, City School or Allied Schools.

## School administrators / chains: procurement, B2B pricing, data protection, integration, offline

### Takeaway
Pakistani edtech has mostly sold B2B to schools, and Beaconhouse has bought system-wide edtech (Knowledge Platform, 100,000 students) and run single-campus pilots, but no per-student B2B prices are published. Infrastructure is the hard constraint: Gallup Pakistan reports computer labs in 3% of government and 17% of private schools, and internet in 7% vs 32% of primary schools. Rahyaab must therefore run on phones, offline or in low-bandwidth conditions. Globally, schools and districts that use structured procurement increasingly require data-privacy terms and interoperability.

### Cited Findings
- Pakistani edtech companies initially started with B2B models, selling services to schools based on their needs — [ProPakistani 2021](https://propakistani.pk/2021/09/17/business-models-of-edtech-companies-in-pakistan/); Queno targets schools that have internet access — [Startup.pk](https://startup.pk/queno-an-edtech-revolutionary-in-the-education-sector-of-pakistan/)
- Beaconhouse signed a system-wide content and technology deal with Knowledge Platform covering about 100,000 students, including O/A-level; financial terms were not disclosed — [Knowledge Platform](https://knowledgeplatform.com/beaconhouse-and-knowledge-platform-sign-education-technology-deal-for-100000-students/). AI Buddy ran a structured pilot at one Beaconhouse campus in Lahore (vendor case study) — [Tutopiya/AI Buddy](https://www.tutopiya.com/blog/ai-buddy/beaconhouse-cantt-campus-lahore-ai-buddy-case-study/). Wateen is rolling out SD-WAN and Wi-Fi across Beaconhouse campuses (infrastructure) — [Digital Pakistan](https://digitalpakistan.pk/wateen-and-beaconhouse-power-up-pakistans-digital-classrooms/)
- Infrastructure (Gallup Pakistan, reported June 2026): internet in 7% of government vs 32% of private primary schools; computer labs in 3% vs 17% — [ProPakistani 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/06/24/30-of-govt-schools-in-pakistan-have-one-teacher-managing-multiple-classrooms/). ASER 2023 (rural) reportedly puts government secondary schools at 51% with labs and 43% with internet, as quoted by a secondary site — [State of Children](https://stateofchildren.com/use-of-technology-in-the-classroom/). In Sindh, 406 of 499 government secondary and higher-secondary schools reportedly have no computer lab — [State of Children](https://stateofchildren.com/use-of-technology-in-the-classroom/). The sources use different definitions and populations.
- Global procurement: Project Unicorn's State of the Sector (Oct 2025) found a clear link between structured procurement processes and the inclusion of interoperability and data-privacy requirements — [ISTE five pillars / Project Unicorn](https://iste.org/blog/the-five-pillars-of-edtech-procurement). Digital Promise's EdTech Quality Indicators include a "SAFE" indicator requiring robust data privacy and security — [Digital Promise EQC](https://digitalpromise.org/EQC). A Digital Promise survey cited by ISTE found 67% saw the lack of data interoperability as a widespread problem (older data) — [ISTE/Project Unicorn buying guide](https://iste.org/news/iste-and-project-unicorn-release-buying-guide-to-help-educators-make-better-technology-purchasing-decisions). CoSN's U.S. State of EdTech 2026 surveyed edtech leaders (Jan–Mar 2026) and covers data privacy and PII policy; I could not verify specific figures — [CoSN 2026](https://www.cosn.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/U.S.-State-of-EdTech-2026.pdf). A vendor procurement rubric lists alignment, data ownership, LMS compatibility and single sign-on — [Pear Deck](https://www.peardeck.com/blog/using-a-procurement-rubric-for-effective-edtech-selection-and-data-interoperability)
- Mindler's Indian school partners are mostly elite private schools (Welham Boys', La Martiniere Girls', CMS, Greenwood High) — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/weekender/this-platform-provides-a-one-stop-solution-to-career-guidance-mindler)

### Inferences
- The first B2B buyers are likely mid-to-premium private chains and their individual campuses. Elite chains already have counsellors and partners, and buy through system-level pilots first. Expect a single-campus pilot before any chain-wide contract.
- Requirements for administrators: a written data-processing agreement (no ads or profiling, data deletion, school owns the data), parental consent workflow, Excel/CSV export (no SIS standard dominates in Pakistan), a branded parent report, and an offline-first mobile app that syncs when connectivity is available.

### Gaps
- No published per-student or per-school B2B prices for Pakistani edtech; none for City School, Allied Schools or PEIMA procurement.
- No information found on which SIS products Pakistani chains use, or on any requirements they publish for data protection from vendors.
- The official AEPAM Pakistan Education Statistics 2023-24 ICT figures were not retrieved.

## Government/NGO channels in Pakistan

### Takeaway
Pakistan has no school-level career-guidance programme comparable to India's Punjab. Public channels are fragmented: NAVTTC runs career counselling centres for TVET and employment, PEF works with edtech partners under school outsourcing, and federal pilots (Taleemabad/NIETE) show the Ministry of Education will pilot edtech in public schools, mostly in Islamabad. Partnerships would come through these routes as pilots.

### Cited Findings
- NAVTTC's PM Youth Skills Development Programme includes a National Employment Exchange Tool with a job portal and career counselling centres; a model centre in Karachi (2019, EU/Germany/Norway-supported), with similar centres in other cities — [TVET Reform](https://tvetreform.org.pk/career-counselling-and-job-placement/); [TVET Reform Karachi centre](https://tvetreform.org.pk/story-and-highlight/career-counselling-and-job-placement-center-established-at-navttc-karachi/). A 2018 agreement aimed to raise awareness of TVET as a career among school leavers and dropouts — [TVET Reform](https://tvetreform.org.pk/story-and-highlight/career-counselling-job-placement-services-to-be-upgraded/). NAVTTC trained more than 100,000 people in FY2025 — [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1224446)
- A May 2025 Tribune letter: career counselling "remains largely confined to private institutions or urban centres", leaving most public school students without support — [Tribune letter](https://tribune.com.pk/letter/2256855/rain-exposes-rawalpindis-drainage-system) (page URL/title mismatch noted by search; opinion)
- PEF: Taaleem Foundation (not Taleemabad) says it is PEF's official edtech partner under the Public Schools Reorganization Program, managing 58 schools in Khanewal and Rawalpindi — [TF](https://www.tf.edu.pk/?p=1506)
- Federal: the Ministry of Education worked with Taleemabad through NIETE on AI lesson plans, reaching 4,000 teachers and 98,000 students (2024), after an earlier pilot with Telenor in six Model Virtual Schools — [EdTech Hub](https://edtechhub.org/topic-tag/ai/page/4/); [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40151461)
- Punjab context: UNICEF's 2025 Learning Pioneers document names PEPIG/PMIU as approval channels for edtech work; the GPE-funded TALEEM programme plans 32,383 tablets for Punjab public primary schools — [UNICEF](https://www.unicef.org/digitaleducation/media/2046/file)
- KP allocated Rs 36.4 billion for school tech upgrades and new schools (FY2025-26) — [ProPakistani 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/07/09/kp-govt-allocates-rs-364-billion-for-schools-for-tech-upgrades-new-schools-and-more/)
- Beaconhouse partnered with Teach For Pakistan (2020) on educational inequity — [Daily Pakistan](https://en.dailypakistan.com.pk/21-Oct-2020/beaconhouse-group-partners-with-teach-for-pakistan-for-ending-educational-inequity)

### Inferences
- The most realistic public routes: (a) PEF partner schools via edtech partners such as TF; (b) the federal Islamabad Model Schools / NIETE pilot route; (c) NAVTTC/TEVTA, adding a TVET or "technical stream" pathway to recommendations, which would make Rahyaab useful to them; (d) NGOs such as Teach For Pakistan as low-cost classroom pilot partners. Each will likely require free or donor-funded pilots, Urdu content, offline operation, and evidence of impact (pre/post decision confidence, e.g., CDSE).
- Indian Punjab's model (teacher career mentors + AI career labs + psychometric tests for girls) is the nearest regional template to cite in pitches.

### Gaps
- No 2025–26 Pakistani provincial (Punjab SED, Sindh, KP) school career-counselling policy or programme found.
- No requirements found from PEF, PEIMA, TEVTA, Teach For Pakistan or Ilm Ideas for deploying a guidance tool; Ilm Ideas was not researched due to budget.
