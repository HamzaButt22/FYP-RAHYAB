# Competitive Landscape: Career Guidance / Stream Selection for Secondary Students (Pakistan, India, Global)

Research date: 2026-10-29. Note: About 18 tool calls were used. Several vendor pages returned errors (e.g. merafuture.pk returned HTTP 500), and many pricing figures come from third-party or competitor comparison pages. These are flagged where relevant.

## Pakistan: who exists, how students currently choose streams, and the evidence on mismatch, parental pressure and the MDCAT funnel

### Takeaway
Pakistan has no dominant, well-funded career-guidance or stream-selection app. The market is fragmented: small test-plus-counsellor websites (CareerCoachs, Merafuture, Eduvision), content portals (ilmkidunya), and well-known edtechs focused on exam prep or curriculum (Maqsad, Sabaq, Edkasa, Taleemabad) that have no visible career-assessment product. Evidence shows that school counselling is rare, parents dominate the choice, and the Pre-Medical funnel is heavily oversubscribed (about 140k MDCAT candidates for about 22k seats in 2025). That leaves a clear opening for an engaging, localized, parent-inclusive product.

### Cited Findings
**Players**
- **CareerCoachs (careercoachs.com/pakistan):**
  - Offers a free AI psychometric test, which the page says takes about 30 minutes. It matches users to 300+ careers with compatibility scores and also lists a "Stream Selection Test".
  - Targets Matric (SSC), FSc/FA (HSSC), MDCAT, ECAT, CSS, NTS and HEC HAT. Available in English, Urdu and Punjabi.
  - Pricing tiers:
    - Free: PKR 0, basic report.
    - Professional: PKR 1,700 (about US$6), detailed report plus a 30-minute counsellor video session. Marked "Most Popular".
    - Premium: about US$12, full report, 60-minute session and roadmap. The FAQ says premium packages start at PKR 3,000, which conflicts with the USD price.
  - Claims 500+ counsellors.
  - [CareerCoachs](https://careercoachs.com/online-career-counselling/pakistan)
- **Merafuture (merafuture.pk):** Markets an "AI Based Aptitude Testing and Career Counselling" service and a Multidimensional Career Counselling Test (MCCT) aimed at intermediate-level students. The site returned HTTP 500 on fetch, so pricing and length could not be verified. — [Merafuture](https://merafuture.pk/); [MCCT page](https://merafuture.pk/mcct-2/)
- **Eduvision (eduvision.edu.pk):** Offers a free online career-counselling aptitude/career-planning test. — [Eduvision](https://www.eduvision.edu.pk/members/home.php)
- **ilmkidunya:** A large education portal (past papers, notes, admissions news, career guidance articles). It also publishes MDCAT news, for example "UHS MDCAT 2025 result: 83% candidates qualify". No evidence was found of a validated career-assessment product. — [ilmkidunya MDCAT news](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/edunews/uhs-mdcat-2025-result-announced--83-candidates-qualify-for-mbbs)
- **Maqsad:** Self-described as "Pakistan's leading online test preparation platform" for MDCAT, ECAT, BCAT and O Level. It is test prep, not stream or career assessment. — [Maqsad](https://maqsad.io/)
- **Sabaq.pk:** A free video-lecture platform for grades 1–12 on the Pakistani syllabus. No career-guidance offering was found. — [search summary of listing](https://www.edumentor.pk/blog/blog_detail/40)
- **Edkasa:** Exam prep for Matric/FSc board exams. Its only career link found is a 2019 LUMS careers session led by its CEO. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/edkasa/alternatives-competitors); [LUMS event](https://old.lums.edu.pk/events/attend-session-%E2%80%98how-explore-progressive-careers%E2%80%99)
- **Taleemabad:** Islamabad-based, founded 2015, curriculum-aligned, about 1.5M downloads. It raised US$2.3M seed (2023). No career-guidance product was found. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2023/09/25/edtech-platform-taleemabad-raises-2-3-million-in-seed-funding/amp/); [Tutopiya](https://www.tutopiya.com/blog/igcse-keywords/taleemabad-alternative-igcse-pakistan/)
- **Career Compass Pakistan (GitHub prototype):**
  - An open-source Streamlit/CrewAI app for intermediate students, not a commercial product.
  - Runs a 10-question assessment and weights fit as 40% interest, 35% aptitude and 25% Pakistan-landscape alignment.
  - Shows that hobbyists and students are building in this space.
  - [GitHub](https://github.com/ikhosa/Career-Compass-Pakistan)
- **Blog and SEO guides** (Parhlai, talib.pk, TME, studentvisaguide, pigment.is) give generic "what to pick after Matric" advice covering Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, ICS, I.Com and FA. One says Pre-Medical typically needs 60–65% in Matric science subjects, and that matric marks count for 10–30% of the university aggregate at many institutions. — [Parhlai](https://parhlai.com/blog/how-to-choose-career-after-matric); [talib.pk](https://talib.pk/career-counseling-in-pakistan/); [TME](https://tme.edu.pk/how-to-choose-the-right-matric-subjects-in-pakistan/); [pigment.is](https://pigment.is/blogs/blogs/career-counseling-for-students-in-pakistan)

**How students choose: counselling scarcity and parental pressure**
- Only 36.6% of surveyed high-school students reported having career-counselling sessions at school, and 82.6% relied on parents' advice for career choice. This is a single study, so treat it as indicative. — [IJCMPH, Arif et al.](https://www.ijcmph.com/index.php/ijcmph/article/view/1435); [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/317867726_Career_counselling_and_its_knowledge_among_high_school_students_in_Pakistan)
- About 30% of students are "clearly not allowed to choose a career on their own". In Karachi, counselling "prevails only in a few private sector institutions". — [Career Counselling Approaches in Pakistani Students](https://researchgate.net/publication/354355591_Career_Counselling_Approaches_in_Pakistani_Students); [Karachi stakeholders study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/407518998_Educational_Career_Counselling_for_the_Higher_Secondary_Level_Students_of_Karachi_Stakeholders_Perspective)
- Named barriers include financial constraints, lack of research and information, and a lack of guidance platforms and services. — [PLHR, Challenges to Career Counseling in Pakistan](https://ojs.plhr.org.pk/journal/article/download/562/459/786)
- Abidi & Malik (2020) interviewed Pakistani career practitioners. They found career counselling neglected, little practitioner training or local tool development, and heavy reliance on foreign theories. They call for an indigenous framework. — [New Horizons 14(2)](https://greenwichjournals.com/index.php/NH/article/view/444)
- **Medicine as a case study of mismatch:**
  - **JPMA 2025, Nishtar Medical University:** 69.5% of first-year MBBS students named parental pressure as the main reason for choosing medicine, versus 23.6% passion. Only 50.6% were fully satisfied and 42.5% were dissatisfied. — [JPMA](https://www.jpma.org.pk/index.php/public_html/article/view/20903)
  - **Shaheen et al. 2021, JRMC:** Nearly a fifth of medical students had no personal interest in medicine and enrolled only because of their parents. — [JRMC](https://journalrmc.com/index.php/JRMC/article/view/415)
  - **Bacha Khan Medical College 2021:** Family pressure was the main driver, and the authors linked low interest to poor performance. — [JKCD](https://journalofkcd.com/kcd/article/view/95)

**MDCAT funnel (2025)**
- More than 140,000 candidates registered for MDCAT 2025 (held 26 Oct 2025) for about 22,000 medical and dental seats. — [The Pakistan Connect](https://thepakistanconnect.com/?p=6035); [PMDC press release](https://pmdc.pk/Documents/press/Press%20release%20MDCAT%202025.pdf)
- Regional turnout:
  - Punjab (UHS): 50,461 appeared, of whom 36,702 were female. A UHS notice gives 47,721 instead.
  - Sindh: 32,531 appeared.
  - KP: 39,986 appeared.
  - [Pakistan Observer](https://pakobserver.net/punjab-conducts-mdcat-under-elaborate-security/); [KMU](https://kmu.edu.pk/news/govt-kp-and-kmu-successfully-conduct-mdcat-2025-across-khyber-pakhtunkhwa); [ilmkidunya](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/edunews/uhs-mdcat-2025-result-announced--83-candidates-qualify-for-mbbs)
- MDCAT 2025 eligibility was raised to 65%. — [Dental Tribune PK](https://pk.dental-tribune.com/news/eligibility-for-mdcat-2025-mdcat-raised-to-65-limits-exam-to-domicile-regions/)

### Inferences
- Roughly 6 in 7 MDCAT candidates (about 118k of 140k) will not get a medical or dental seat. Many of them committed to Pre-Medical at age 15–16, often because of parental pressure. This is the clearest stream-mismatch pain point and a strong marketing hook ("explore before you lock in Pre-Med").
- Female candidates make up about 73% of Punjab MDCAT takers (36.7k of 50.5k). This suggests the stream choice is gendered and that a product should address families of girls specifically.
- Pakistani competitors are mostly questionnaire plus counsellor-upsell sites priced at roughly PKR 0–3,000. None was found that is game-based, mobile-first and app-store distributed with meaningful scale. Major edtechs (Maqsad, Taleemabad, Edkasa, Sabaq) could add career modules and are the most credible future competitors or distribution partners.
- O/A Level students are served by international platforms (Cialfo, study-abroad counsellors) more than local tools. No Pakistan-specific O/A Level subject-selection tool was found.

### Gaps
- No user-count, revenue or app-store review data was found for any Pakistani career-guidance player. CareerCoachs, Merafuture and Eduvision ratings could not be verified.
- No reliable source was found for Pakistan's Punjab (or any provincial) government school career-guidance programme. Search results were dominated by Indian Punjab's 2025 "AI career guidance labs" with Beyond Mentor (25 labs, free to students), which is a useful model but not Pakistan. — [The Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/punjab-launches-ai-driven-career-guidance-in-government-schools/)
- Several items could not be confirmed: HEC career-counselling initiatives, Teach for Pakistan or Alif Ailaan career programmes, ASER data on career guidance, CareerPakistan/CareerHub, Tutorbridge, and any Gulf/UAE-based apps targeting Pakistani students.
- No national data on how Matric students split across streams (Pre-Med, Pre-Eng, ICS, I.Com, FA), and no ECAT 2025 applicant numbers.
- No independent check of Merafuture's MCCT (length, price, validation).

## India: Mindler, iDreamCareer, Univariety, CareerGuide and others (tests, pricing, B2B)

### Takeaway
The Indian market is mature and is the closest analogue: psychometric questionnaire batteries (Mindler's 5-dimension, 56-trait battery taking about 2.5 hours; Edumilestones' 5 dimensions) sold B2C at ₹1,500–25,000+ with counsellor-session upsells, and B2B through schools. Stream selection after Class 10 (Science, Commerce, Arts) is a core product everywhere.

### Cited Findings
- **Mindler:**
  - The assessment covers 5 dimensions (orientation style, interest, aptitude, personality, socio-emotional intelligence), per a published validation paper. Partner schools describe it as evaluating "56 holistic dimensions" and producing best-fit career and stream matches. — [Asia Pacific Career Development Journal](https://asiapacificcda.org/Resources/APCDJ/A0006_1_01.pdf); [Ramagya School](https://ramagyaschool.com/dadri/mindler)
  - It has a stream-selector assessment for grades 8–10. A partner cites a 97% "accuracy index", which is unverified vendor marketing. — [Cialfo blog](https://www.cialfo.co/blog/?p=1201)
  - The test takes about 2.5 hours.
  - Plans: Learn ₹2,400; Explore ₹11,400; Realize ₹25,000+. These figures come from a competitor comparison checked on 19 Oct 2026. — [MakeMyCareer.AI](https://www.makemycareer.ai/guides/best-career-guidance-platforms-2026)
  - Business model: B2B through schools and institutions, B2C to parents and students, and strategic partnerships. It raised US$1.5M pre-Series A (2021) and is integrated into Cialfo's school counselling platform. — [BW Disrupt](https://bwdisrupt.com/article/mindler-a-technology-driven-system-that-helps-students-to-choose-the-career-path-best-suitable-for-them-98537); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2021/09/funding-alert-edtech-startup-mindler-raises-1-5-million-pre-series-a-funding/amp)
  - Mindler's CEO has publicly said Indian students choose careers under parental pressure. — [BusinessWorld](https://businessworld.in/article/students-choosing-career-options-under-parental-pressure-ceo-mindler--167228)
  - Mindler runs school student and parent orientation sessions, an example of the parent-involvement B2B model. — [Jaipuria](https://sajk.jaipuria.edu.in/mindler-student-parent-orientation-session/)
- **iDreamCareer:**
  - Career Path Pro targets classes 9–10 stream choice.
  - Prices are not published. Older or third-party figures conflict: from ₹2,700 (2021), ₹3,600–9,000, a ₹1,500 test, and a ₹1,500–15,000 range.
  - [iDreamCareer blog](https://idreamcareer.com/blog/best-career-aptitude-test/); [Medium 2021](https://careercounselling.medium.com/7-best-career-assessment-test-in-india-f876587b0803); [iDreamCareer competitor landscape PDF](https://idreamcareer.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Full-Competitor-Landscape.pdf)
- **Univariety:** Its stream selector assesses interests, aptitude and skills and recommends Science, Commerce or Arts after Class 10. A 2021 price of ₹2,360 was found; no current price. — [Univariety](https://www.univariety.com/resources/featured-insights/detail.php?name=psychometric-tests); [Medium 2021](https://careercounselling.medium.com/7-best-career-assessment-test-in-india-f876587b0803)
- **CareerGuide:** ₹999 report only; ₹5,999 for 2 tests plus 1 video session; ₹10,000 per counselling session. — [MakeMyCareer.AI](https://www.makemycareer.ai/guides/best-career-guidance-platforms-2026)
- **Edumilestones:** 5 dimensions (personality, interests, motivators, learning style, skills/abilities). Comprehensive plan ₹5,999, free one-path report, face-to-face from ₹2,000. — [MakeMyCareer.AI](https://www.makemycareer.ai/guides/best-career-guidance-platforms-2026)
- **MakeMyCareer.AI (AI-only, no human counsellor):**
  - Plans run from ₹199 to ₹4,999, one-time payment.
  - Payments are non-refundable.
  - [MakeMyCareer.AI](https://www.makemycareer.ai/guides/best-career-guidance-platforms-2026)
- **Buddy4Study:** Stream selector at ₹99, the cheapest benchmark found. — [search result summary, vhtc.org/obcrights](https://www.vhtc.org/2025/02/stream-selector-tests-after-10th.html)
- **Careers360:** A free college, course and exam information portal, not an assessment. — [MakeMyCareer.AI](https://www.makemycareer.ai/guides/best-career-guidance-platforms-2026)
- **Government (Indian Punjab, 2025):**
  - 25 AI career-guidance labs in government schools with Beyond Mentor.
  - Free to students and the state.
  - Shows a public-sector B2G channel.
  - [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/punjab-launches-ai-driven-career-guidance-in-government-schools/)

### Inferences
- The Indian price ladder has three rungs:
  - Cheap test: ₹99–2,400.
  - Test plus counsellor: ₹5–11k.
  - Premium mentoring: ₹25k+.
- Converted to PKR at rough purchasing-power parity, the CareerCoachs tiers (PKR 0–3,000) sit at the bottom rung. A Pakistani app likely needs a free or cheap gamified core with paid parent reports or counsellor upsells.
- A 2.5-hour Mindler test confirms that long questionnaires are the norm. Shorter, game-based assessment is a differentiator.

### Gaps
- No current verified pricing was found for iDreamCareer, Univariety, Career Launcher, CareerAnna, Jobs.ai or BYJU's career offerings. Jobs.ai and CareerAnna were not researched for lack of tool budget.
- No per-student B2B school price was found for any Indian player.
- No Google Play review analysis was found for Indian career apps. A search for "Mindler reviews" returned the unrelated Swedish/UK mental-health company Mindler (mindlercare.com), which shares the name. Do not confuse the two.

## Global: YouScience, Naviance, Xello, Kuder, O*NET, CareerExplorer and gamified tools (games vs questionnaires, length, pricing)

### Takeaway
YouScience Brightpath is the main commercial game-based (aptitude "brain games") player, and it is US school-licensed. Most others (Naviance, Xello, Kuder, O*NET, CareerExplorer, 16Personalities) are questionnaires. Teen-focused gamified career assessment is mostly academic prototypes or small apps, so no scaled game-first consumer product exists, especially in South Asia.

### Cited Findings
- **YouScience Brightpath:**
  - Uses up to 12 brain games to measure aptitudes, plus an interests component. The report covers aptitudes, interests and working style.
  - Serves middle school, high school, higher education and workforce. Pricing is "contact sales".
  - Offers 200+ industry certifications.
  - The games are timed.
  - [YouScience](https://www.youscience.com/education/); [Fort Dodge schools](https://www.fdschools.org/article/1750817)
  - An estimated US$25–45 per student, from a third-party competitor blog. — [JobCannon](https://jobcannon.io/compare/top-5-k12-career-platforms)
- **Naviance (PowerSchool):**
  - About US$5–12 per student per year, a third-party estimate. EdSurge reportedly cited about US$10 per student.
  - Interest assessment plus college-application tools.
  - [CareerClutch](https://careerclutch.ai/blog/naviance-comparison-guide-features-compliance-gaps.html); [futuremeanswered](https://www.futuremeanswered.com/naviance-alternatives)
- **Xello:** About US$4–8 per student (estimate), quote-based. Assessments cover interests and working style. Florida districts reportedly get it free through a state contract. Students cannot sign up individually. — [futuremeanswered Xello review](https://www.futuremeanswered.com/xello-review); [CareerClutch](https://careerclutch.ai/blog/xello-comparison-guide-career-readiness-platforms.html)
- **Kuder:** Offers interest, skills-confidence and work-values instruments. Price is not published. — [pigment.is](https://pigment.is/blogs/blogs/kuder-career-planning-system)
- **O*NET Interest Profiler / My Next Move (US Dept of Labor):**
  - Free RIASEC questionnaire: 60 items, or a 30-item mobile Mini-IP.
  - Takes about 10–20 minutes.
  - Widely embedded in other platforms, such as Pathful.
  - [O*NET Center](https://www.onetcenter.org/IP.html); [O*NET reference](https://services.onetcenter.org/reference/mnm/ip/ip_questions); [Pathful](https://helpcenter.pathful.com/articles/5188654-onet-interest-profiler-onet-ip)
- **CareerExplorer (Sokanu, acquired by Penn Foster in March 2021):**
  - Questionnaire taking under about 20 minutes on average (older source).
  - Free core, with about a US$35 upgrade for the full report (unverified third party).
  - Matches across 700+ careers.
  - Praised on Reddit-sourced roundups.
  - [Yaletown](https://yaletown.com/news/yaletown-congratulates-portfolio-company-sokanu-on-sale-of-careerexplorer-to-penn-foster); [capitalandmain](https://capitalandmain.com/if-sokanu-tells-you-to-consider-becoming-a-florist-youd-be-wise-to-listen-0405); [UNR](https://www.unr.edu/career/faculty-resources/career-exploration); [Zety](https://zety.com/blog/best-career-test)
- **JobCannon:**
  - Flat US$199/month for up to 200 assessments.
  - JobCannon publishes the comparison pages cited above, so it has a commercial interest.
  - [JobCannon](https://jobcannon.io/compare/top-5-k12-career-platforms)
- **Gamified examples:**
  - Pymetrics originally pitched games for high schoolers in 2014. — [NYU News](https://nyunews.com/2014/09/17/pymetrics)
  - **TraitsNavigator Quest (UiTM Malaysia 2025):** A gamified psychometric prototype built explicitly to reduce the anxiety and tedium of long repetitive tests. — [UiTM](https://ir.uitm.edu.my/id/eprint/126857/1/126857.pdf)
  - **Kazakhstan 2025:** A 3D game with a hidden (stealth) assessment of high-schoolers' ICT skills, which motivates students without adding testing time. — [Frontiers in Education](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2025.1643246/pdf)
  - **G-GUIDANCE (EU, 2019–22):** A gamified platform for ages 13–17. — [Univ. Coimbra](https://www.uc.pt/en/ceis20/externally-funded-projects/g-guidance)
  - **Teen Career Path:** An iOS app with paid membership. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1382949210)
  - **YouReGa:** An RPG-style career test covering 268 careers. — [PeerPush](https://peerpush.com/p/yourega-fun-career-test-rpg)
  - **Simcoach Games:** Career-training games. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/simcoach-games-ceo-jessica-trybus-will-discuss-how-to-gamify-career-training-at-blueprint-york/)

### Inferences
- **Games vs questionnaires:**
  - Games: YouScience (aptitude games), plus prototypes and small apps.
  - Questionnaires: everyone else, including all Indian and Pakistani players found.
  - A game-based Pakistani app would be first-of-kind locally and would echo YouScience's main differentiator: measuring aptitude by performance rather than self-report.
- Global tools give career or college output, not Pakistan stream-level advice (Pre-Med, Pre-Eng, ICS, I.Com, FA), and they are not localized for Urdu or the Pakistani board and MDCAT/ECAT context.
- B2B school per-seat pricing globally (US$4–45 per student per year) is far above Pakistani willingness to pay. Pakistani B2B would need low per-student pricing or sponsorship (government, NGO, UNICEF-style).

### Gaps
- No official YouScience test duration was found; vendor pages omit it.
- No data was found on Roadmap, Morrisby, Pathful pricing, IBM SkillsBuild or CareerVillage specifics, or the official 16Personalities question count (third-party results conflicted: 20–96 items).
- No independent validation of game-based versus questionnaire predictive accuracy was gathered.

## User complaints, gaps and business-model implications

### Takeaway
Direct app-store or Reddit complaint data for career-guidance tools was sparse in this search. The documented and inferable gaps are:
- long tests (about 2.5 hours for Mindler);
- paywalled full reports (CareerExplorer about US$35, CareerGuide ₹999, CareerCoachs PKR 1,700+);
- no individual access to school-only platforms (Xello);
- reliance on foreign, non-localized frameworks in Pakistan;
- weak parent involvement despite parents being the main decision-makers.

### Cited Findings
- Researchers built gamified career assessment specifically because long, repetitive psychometric tests cause anxiety and tedium. — [UiTM TraitsNavigator](https://ir.uitm.edu.my/id/eprint/126857/1/126857.pdf)
- Xello cannot be tried by individuals; it is district-licensed only. — [futuremeanswered](https://www.futuremeanswered.com/xello-review)
- CareerExplorer is criticized for lacking integration with real-time job-market data (low-quality editorial source). — [insidea](https://insidea.com/spotlight/listing/careerexplorer/)
- Pakistani practice relies on foreign theories and tools, with little indigenous development. — [Abidi & Malik 2020](https://greenwichjournals.com/index.php/NH/article/view/444)
- 82.6% of Pakistani students rely on parents' career advice, and only 36.6% get school counselling. — [IJCMPH](https://www.ijcmph.com/index.php/ijcmph/article/view/1435)
- Paywalls and upsell patterns are visible in the pricing of CareerCoachs, CareerGuide, Mindler and MakeMyCareer.AI (non-refundable). — [CareerCoachs](https://careercoachs.com/online-career-counselling/pakistan); [MakeMyCareer.AI](https://www.makemycareer.ai/guides/best-career-guidance-platforms-2026)

### Inferences
Opportunities for a game-based Pakistani app:
1. **Short, playable assessment:** Mobile mini-games for aptitude, totalling well under 30–45 minutes, replacing 2.5-hour questionnaires.
2. **Pakistan-specific output:** A direct recommendation among Pre-Med, Pre-Eng, ICS, I.Com, FA/Humanities and O/A-Level subject combinations, linked to MDCAT/ECAT odds (about 1 in 6 MDCAT seat odds) and realistic local careers.
3. **Language:** Urdu-first with English, plus possibly regional languages (CareerCoachs already offers Punjabi).
4. **A parent mode or report** designed to shift parental pressure with evidence.
5. **Freemium with a cheap paid tier:** Benchmark at or below PKR 1,700 for a counsellor session.
6. **B2B:** Low-cost school or NGO/government licences, following the free Indian Punjab labs model.
7. **Distribution:** Partnerships with prep edtechs (Maqsad, Edkasa) that already own the Pre-Med and Pre-Eng audience.

### Gaps
- No Google Play review mining was possible for Pakistani or Indian career apps. Specific user complaints ("generic results", "boring") are inferred, not quoted.
- No Reddit or r/pakistan threads on career tests were surfaced.
- No data on willingness to pay among Pakistani parents for career guidance.
