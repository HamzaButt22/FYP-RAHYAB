# Pakistan Secondary Stream Structure (Classes 9-12) and Trait-to-Stream/Course Mapping

Research date: 29 Oct 2026. About 15 tool calls. Many hits were aggregator or education blogs (ilmkidunya, maqsad, parhlai, result.pk); these are marked. Official board PDFs were partly unreachable (one BISE Lahore PDF returned a 404).

## Matric (SSC, classes 9-10) groups, by board, and 2026 curriculum changes

### Takeaway
At SSC there are two main groups: Science (Physics, Chemistry, Maths plus either Biology or Computer Science) and Humanities/Arts (core subjects plus electives). The Biology/CS choice at Class 9 is the first real fork, because Biology leads to Pre-Medical and CS leads to ICS/Pre-Engineering. A revised national "Inclusive Scheme of Studies 2024" (National Curriculum Council) applies to SSC exams from 2025. FBISE is moving STEM papers to 50% MCQ / 50% descriptive from the Second Annual 2026. A Matric "Technical" route also exists, which feeds the DAE.

### Cited Findings
- Revised SSC scheme (National Curriculum Council, reproduced by AKU-EB) is implemented from Annual Examinations 2025. SSC-II science core is Mathematics (Science), Physics, Chemistry, and Biology OR Computer Science (75 marks each). Total scheme is 1,200 marks — [AKU-EB SSC Scheme of Studies 2025 and onwards](https://examinationboard.aku.edu/learning-materials/Documents/SSC%20Scheme%20of%20Studies%202025%20and%20Onwards.pdf)
- The Humanities group under the same scheme has English, Urdu/Sindhi (or History & Geography), Pakistan Studies, and Mathematics (Humanities), plus any three electives. Electives include General Science, Computer Science, Economics, Civics and Geography — [AKU-EB SSC Scheme 2025+](https://examinationboard.aku.edu/learning-materials/Documents/SSC%20Scheme%20of%20Studies%202025%20and%20Onwards.pdf)
- Older FBISE scheme: a 500-mark compulsory block plus a 600-mark optional group. Science Group is Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology / Computer Science / a Technical subject. This is the legacy structure — [FBISE Scheme of Studies SSC](https://mail.fbise.edu.pk/Syllabus/Scheme-of-Studies-SSC.pdf)
- FBISE has a separate notification on Humanities group subjects — [FBISE notification](https://www.fbise.edu.pk/notifications/Notification%20of%20HUMANITIES%20GROUP%20SUBJECTS%206.pdf) (not read in full)
- FBISE's new pattern is 50% MCQ / 50% descriptive in Maths, Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Computer Science, starting with the SSC-I and HSSC-I Second Annual Examination 2026 — [FBISE notification 2026](https://www.fbise.edu.pk/notifications/Notification_of_50_percent_Pattern_2026.pdf); [ProPakistani, Aug 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/08/15/fbise-introduces-new-exam-pattern-for-five-subjects/)
- BISE Lahore (Punjab) publishes 9th/10th syllabus for 2025-27 and a 2024-26 study scheme — [BISE Lahore syllabus 9-10 2025-27](https://www.biselahore.com/downloads/notifications/misc/Removed%20-%20Syllabus_9th&10th_(2025_27).pdf)
- Technical route: "Matric Technical" (e.g., Computer Technology) is entered after Middle (Class 8). DAE admission (Punjab TEVTA) requires Matric Science with Physics, Chemistry, Maths and English. The minimum is 40% in some listings and 50% at some colleges. Merit is based on Matric marks in Maths/Physics/Chemistry/English. Some listings also accept "SSC Science or TSC" (Technical School Certificate) — [result.pk TEVTA DAE 2026](https://www.result.pk/educom/tevta-dae-admissions-2026-details-out-61718.htm); [ilmkidunya GCT Bahawalpur](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/admissions/admission-open-in-govt-college-of-technology-bahwalpur-23679) (aggregators)
- Sindh's STEVTA also runs DAE admissions — [result.pk STEVTA 2025](https://www.result.pk/educom/stevta-admissions-2025-dae-50794.htm)

### Inferences
- In the app, the Class 9 decision should be shown as three choices: Science-Biology, Science-Computer, and Humanities. A Technical/vocational option should be added where it is available. Science-Biology keeps medical and engineering open (with extra Maths later). Science-Computer closes the MBBS route. Humanities closes MBBS and engineering.
- A Matric Humanities student cannot normally join DAE, which needs Matric Science per the TEVTA listings. This is a dead end the app should flag.
- Students with Biology at SSC can still move to ICS or Pre-Engineering at HSSC. Students with CS at SSC cannot move to Pre-Medical, because HSSC Biology needs the SSC Biology foundation. This is the common college practice. It is inferred from the group structure and not confirmed from a board rule.

### Gaps
- No primary source was read for exact current SSC groups at Sindh boards (BSEK), KP BISEs, or the Balochistan board. They follow the same national scheme, but board-specific adoption dates were not verified.
- How far the National Curriculum of Pakistan (formerly SNC) has been implemented at Classes 9-12 was not confirmed beyond the 2024 Inclusive Scheme of Studies.
- The FBISE notification on Humanities subjects was not read in full.

## Intermediate (HSSC, classes 11-12) groups and Cambridge equivalents

### Takeaway
The main HSSC groups are:
- FSc Pre-Medical (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
- FSc Pre-Engineering (Physics, Chemistry, Maths)
- ICS (Maths + CS + Physics, Statistics or Economics)
- General Science (Maths, Statistics, Economics)
- I.Com
- FA/Humanities with elective combinations (FA IT is a common variant)

Alternatives are DAE (a 3-year diploma after Matric Science) and Cambridge A Levels, which need IBCC equivalence.

### Cited Findings
- English, Urdu and Pakistan Studies are compulsory for all FSc and FA students (Punjab college example). The college also offers ICS (Physics) and ICS (Statistics), and General Science with Economics, Maths and Statistics — [Murray College programmes](https://murraycollege.edu.pk/programmes/)
- LCWU's intermediate offerings for 2025-27 are ICS (Physics, Maths, CS), ICS (Statistics, Maths, CS), ICS (Economics, Maths, CS), and General Science (Maths, Statistics, Economics) — [LCWU Intermediate College](https://lcwu.edu.pk/inter-colege-admission.html)
- The BISE Lahore 2024-26 scheme lists permitted elective combinations (choose any one): Physics+Maths+Statistics, Maths+Economics+Statistics, Economics+Maths+CS, Maths+Statistics+CS, and Maths+Physics+CS. BISE Lahore's 2025-27 syllabus also lists "Computer Science and Entrepreneurship" as a subject — [BISE Lahore StudyScheme Inter 2024-26](https://www.biselahore.com/downloads/notifications/misc/StudySchemeInter_2024-26.pdf); [BISE Lahore Syllabus 11-12 2025-27](https://www.biselahore.com/downloads/notifications/misc/Syllabus_11th&12th_(2025-2027).pdf)
- BISE Abbottabad (KP) publishes an HSSC group/subject combination list — [BISE Abbottabad Group Subjects Combination HSSC](https://biseatd.edu.pk/uploads/files/Group_Subjects_Combination_HSSC.pdf) (not read in full)
- FBISE HSSC has separate syllabi for Commerce Group, Psychology, Sociology, Education, Statistics, Library Science and Islamic History. These show the range of FA electives — [FBISE Commerce Group](https://mail.fbise.edu.pk/Syllabus/HSSC-I/Commerce%20Group/Commerce%20Group.pdf); [FBISE Psychology](https://mail.fbise.edu.pk/Syllabus/HSSC-II/Psychology/Psychology.doc)
- FA IT is described as CS + Economics + Civics, and FA General Science as Maths + Statistics + Economics. The exact combination varies by board and college. Source is a third-party blog only — [Parhlai FA subjects](https://parhlai.com/blog/fa-subjects)
- A-Level students need an IBCC equivalence certificate. For MBBS, this must show at least 60% in pre-medical subjects — [ilmkidunya PMDC clarification](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/edunews/pmdc-clarifies-fsc-marks-requirement-for-mbbs-bds-admissions); [RMI Admissions 2025-26](https://admissions.rmi.edu.pk/admissions-2025-26/)

### Inferences
Suggested HSSC options for the app:

| HSSC option | Typical trait profile | Main doors it opens |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-Medical | Investigative + Social; strong Biology/Chemistry | MBBS, BDS, Pharm-D, DPT, Nursing, allied health, Biosciences |
| Pre-Engineering | Investigative + Realistic; strong Maths/Physics | Engineering, BSCS, Architecture, Physics/Maths |
| ICS | Investigative + Conventional; strong Maths/logic | BSCS/SE/AI/DS, engineering per PEC |
| I.Com | Conventional + Enterprising | BBA, B.Com, ACCA/CA, Accounting & Finance |
| FA (by electives) | Artistic/Social/Enterprising; verbal strength | LLB, Psychology, Media, Fine Arts, Education, Social Sciences, CSS route |
| General Science | Quantitative but not lab-oriented | Economics, Statistics, BBA, and in some cases BSCS |

- Cambridge students: choose A Level Biology/Chemistry/Physics for the medical route, Maths/Physics (plus Chemistry or CS) for engineering, and other combinations for business or humanities. All routes need IBCC equivalence.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 HSSC group list was read for Sindh (BIEK), Balochistan or FBISE as a full list. FA IT composition was confirmed only from a blog.
- D.Com (the commerce diploma from technical boards) was not researched.

## Downstream programs per stream, entry rules, switching and dead-ends

### Takeaway
There is no single national rule mapping each HSSC group to each degree. HEC sets a general floor (typically 45% / second division) and universities or councils add program rules. The professional councils are the binding constraints:
- **PMDC (MBBS/BDS):** 60% in FSc Pre-Medical plus MDCAT 55% (MBBS) or 50% (BDS).
- **PEC (engineering):** 60% in HSSC with Pre-Engineering, ICS or relevant DAE. Pre-Medical students need extra Maths.

Switching is possible mainly in the Pre-Medical → engineering/computing direction, through additional Maths. Medicine requires Biology.

### Cited Findings
**General HEC and university floors**
- HEC says applicants must meet "HEC and university criteria". Program eligibility is set by institutions — [HEC FAQs](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/CRHES/Pages/FAQs.aspx)
- Typical floor: 45% in HSSC/A-Level (IBCC-certified) for BS CS, Biosciences and English (Jinnah University example). Third division is ineligible at some universities — [Jinnah University eligibility](https://admissions.jinnah.edu/?p=391618); [ilmkidunya University of Buner](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/colleges/university-of-buner-admission.aspx)
- Example of a group-specific rule: BS Botany at WUS requires FSc Pre-Medical with at least 45% — [WUS Revised Eligibility Fall 2024](https://wus.edu.pk/uploads/student-corner/1.admission/Revised_Eligibility_Criteria_Session_Fall_2024.pdf)

**MBBS/BDS (PMDC and MDCAT)**
- PMDC clarified in October 2026 that the minimum is 60% in FSc Pre-Medical or equivalent. There were conflicting 2025 reports of a rise to 65% for MDCAT 2026 onward, which is unresolved — [ilmkidunya PMDC clarification](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/edunews/pmdc-clarifies-fsc-marks-requirement-for-mbbs-bds-admissions); [Minute Mirror](https://minutemirror.com.pk/pmdc-clarifies-fsc-requirement-for-mbbs-bds-admissions-649753/); [ProPakistani, Oct 2025, new rules MDCAT 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2025/10/21/pmdc-introduces-new-rules-for-mdcat-2026-onwards/amp/)
- MDCAT pass marks are 55% for MBBS and 50% for BDS. Commonly cited merit weights are MDCAT 50%, FSc 40%, Matric 10% (the weights come from a calculator site) — [SZABMU eligibility](https://admission.szabmu.edu.pk/public/eligibility.php); [uni2study calculator](https://www.uni2study.com/pakistan-mdcat-aggregate-master-pmc-pmdc-calculator)
- MDCAT 2025 (held 26 Oct 2025) had 140,071 registered candidates for about 22,000 medical and dental seats — [PMDC press release MDCAT 2025](https://pmdc.pk/Documents/press/Press%20release%20MDCAT%202025.pdf); [Dental Tribune](https://pk.dental-tribune.com/news/mdcat-2025-held-successfully-across-pakistan-under-pmdc-supervision/)
- KP (KMU) results: 53.26% scored 55% or more (MBBS-eligible) and 58.43% scored 50% or more (BDS) — [Dawn: 53pc qualify](https://www.dawn.com/news/1951611); [TNN](https://tnnenglish.com/kmu-announces-mdcat-2025-results-over-53-qualify-for-mbbs)
- Punjab (UHS): about 50,461 candidates appeared (36,702 female, 13,759 male) — [Daily Pakistan](https://en.dailypakistan.com.pk/26-Oct-2025/uhs-mdcat-2025-check-paper-and-answer-keys-for-26-october-2025)
- PMDC allowed a reduction of up to 3 percentage points in the MDCAT pass mark for session 2025-26, to fill vacant seats. This was the second year in a row it lowered merit — [ProPakistani Apr 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/04/09/mdcat-passing-percentage-reduced-by-3/amp/); [The News](https://www.thenews.pk/print/1408981-pmdc-lowers-mdcat-merit-for-second-consecutive-year-to-fill-vacant-seats)

**Engineering (PEC, ECAT, NUST NET)**
- PEC requires 60% in HSSC for engineering admission. ICS (Physics, Maths, CS) students are eligible for all engineering programs. Some universities add Chemistry as a first-semester remedial course for ICS students. DAE holders with at least 60% are eligible for the relevant engineering programs, from PEC's list of accepted technologies — [DUET eligibility](https://admissions.duet.edu.pk/eligibility1/); [Eduvision: PEC ICS eligible](https://www.eduvision.edu.pk/edu_news/pec-declares-ics-students-eligible-for-admissions-to-all-engineering-programs-news-3883); [UET Taxila eligibility](https://admissions.uettaxila.edu.pk/Admission_Eligibility.php)
- Pre-Medical students can enter engineering if they take Additional Mathematics, or through a provisional or foundation-maths route — [Eduvision: PEC allows pre-medical](https://eduvision.edu.pk/edu_news/pec-allows-pre-medical-students-to-seek-admissions-to-engineering-programs-news-4086)
- NUST rules for Pre-Medical students:
  - With Additional Maths, they can apply for engineering, with admission provisional until the result.
  - Without it, they need an 8-week condensed Maths course for Engineering and Robotics/AI.
  - Computing requires 6 credit hours of deficiency Maths.
  - NET Engineering weights are Maths 50%, Physics 30%, English 20%.
  - Sources are aggregators; [ilmkidunya](https://www.ilmkidunya.com/nust-net-entry-test/eligibility-criteria) cites the 60% SSC/HSSC floor.
  - [PMDC.org.pk NUST guide (unofficial site)](https://pmdc.org.pk/nust-checking/); [Parhlai NUST 2026](https://parhlai.com/blog/nust-admission-2026)

**Pharm-D**
- Pharm-D is a 5-year program. It requires at least 60% in FSc Pre-Medical (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) or equivalent, and graduates must register with the Pharmacy Council of Pakistan to practise. Sources conflict on whether MDCAT is required — [PU Pharm.D](https://pu.edu.pk/program/show/3801/Pharm.D-Morning.html); [Maqsad blog](https://blog.maqsad.io/blog/d-pharmacy-in-pakistan-is-mdcat-required)

### Inferences
**Stream-to-program map (from the councils above plus general knowledge; verify per university)**
- **Pre-Medical:** MBBS/BDS, Pharm-D, DPT, BS Nursing, Allied Health (MLT, radiology), Biotech/Microbiology, Agriculture/Veterinary (DVM), Psychology, BBA, LLB. Engineering or CS need extra Maths.
- **Pre-Engineering:** all engineering (ECAT/NET), BSCS/SE/AI, Architecture (plus a drawing or aptitude test), Physics/Maths, BBA, Economics, LLB.
- **ICS:** BSCS/SE/IT/Data Science and engineering (PEC), BBA, Economics. No medicine.
- **I.Com:** BBA, B.Com, BS Accounting & Finance, CA, ACCA, LLB. CS is possible at some universities with deficiency Maths.
- **FA:** LLB (5-year), BS Psychology, Mass Communication, English, IR, Education, Fine Arts (NCA aptitude test), Sociology, Islamic Studies, CSS later. Restricted from medicine, engineering and most CS programs.

**Dead-end handling for Pre-Medical students who fail MDCAT**
- About 140k compete for about 22k seats (roughly 16% admitted, an inference). The app should always show a "Plan B" set that keeps Biology: Pharm-D, DPT, Nursing, Allied Health, Biosciences, Psychology. It should also show that Additional Maths unlocks engineering and CS.

### Gaps
- Primary HEC undergraduate policy text on group eligibility was not found.
- Exact DPT, BS Nursing (PNMC), 5-year LLB (Pakistan Bar Council/HEC law admission test), Architecture and NCA Fine Arts rules were not verified in this session.
- No current figures for CA/ACCA entry, ECAT statistics, FAST/LUMS/IBA eligibility, or a national MDCAT pass rate.

## Trait mapping and Pakistani research on stream-choice factors

### Takeaway
I found no Pakistani validated study mapping RIASEC or aptitude scores to streams. Trait mapping must rely on general RIASEC logic plus Pakistan-specific realities. These realities include strong parental and social prestige pressure toward FSc (doctor or engineer), the stigma of FA as "weak", low access to counselling, and gender norms that reduce girls' STEM participation.

### Cited Findings
- Parents push children into the science stream so they become doctors or engineers, and some students underperform because they couldn't choose — [Gulf News (Pakistani student)](https://gulfnews.com/news/uae/education/dubai-student-battles-odds-with-diligence-1.1459752)
- FSc is viewed as intellectual and FA students as academically weak, so many students take FSc without interest (opinion letter) — [Express Tribune letter](https://tribune.com.pk/letter/2253655/career-counselling)
- Lack of school career counselling leads students to choose subjects without prior knowledge. Writers call for counsellors in every college (opinion) — [Express Tribune letter](https://tribune.com.pk/letter/2066123/student-counseling); [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1597563)
- A Karachi survey of 500 high-school students found only 54.8% knew about career counselling. A counsellor's availability and attending seminars were associated with that knowledge — [IJCMPH study](https://www.ijcmph.com/index.php/ijcmph/article/download/1435/1243/5980)
- Factors in Pakistani students' choices include aptitude, peer pressure, parental influence, curriculum diversity and financial constraints — [INTED 2011 paper](https://library.iated.org/view/ZAFAR2011ACA)
- Gender: interviews with 31 female students found that patriarchal values shaped women's perceptions of their own abilities and aspirations, and teaching styles linked to dropping out of the maths and science pipeline — [Mujtaba & Reiss 2015, UCL](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1474624/)
- Tertiary enrolment is near gender parity, but STEM parity is unmet — [British Council STEM report](https://www.britishcouncil.pk/sites/default/files/stem_final_with_foreword_1_1.pdf)
- Women's science enrolment is lower than arts/humanities, with greater risk for rural and less-privileged girls — [Qaisar 2024, JHETP](https://mail.articlegateway.com/index.php/JHETP/article/download/7324/6915)
- Regional analogue (India): girls were about 20 percentage points less likely to take Science or Commerce, even after controlling for test scores — [Sahoo & Klasen, IIMB](https://research.iimb.ac.in/fac_pubs/86)
- Counterpoint: girls dominate MDCAT. In Punjab, about 36.7k of 50.5k who appeared were female — [Daily Pakistan](https://en.dailypakistan.com.pk/26-Oct-2025/uhs-mdcat-2025-check-paper-and-answer-keys-for-26-october-2025)

### Inferences
**Mapping rules for the app (heuristic, not empirically validated in Pakistan)**
- **Pre-Medical:** Investigative + Social interests, scientific reasoning, memory/verbal, strong SSC Biology and Chemistry (at least 80% is realistic given the 60% floor and competition).
- **Pre-Engineering:** Investigative + Realistic, numerical and spatial aptitude, strong SSC Maths and Physics.
- **ICS:** Investigative + Conventional, numerical/logical reasoning, interest in computers or building things.
- **I.Com:** Conventional + Enterprising, numerical aptitude, interest in business or money.
- **FA:** Artistic, Social or Enterprising, high verbal aptitude. Elective choice should follow the specific interest: Psychology (Social), Fine Arts (Artistic), Economics/Civics (Enterprising), IT (Conventional/Investigative).
- **DAE/technical:** Realistic, hands-on and spatial, with a preference for earning sooner.

**Design implications**
- The app should explicitly counter the prestige bias. Present FA, I.Com and DAE as strong routes with clear outcomes, not as fallbacks.
- Flag gender-norm effects gently, for example by showing girls in engineering and CS examples.

### Gaps
- No Pakistani psychometric validation study linking RIASEC or aptitude scores to stream success.
- No large, recent, nationally representative data on how often parents decide the stream.

## Labour market signals for 2026

### Takeaway
IT is the clearest growth signal. FY26 IT exports were about $4.6bn (+21%), and freelancer earnings hit records. Overall unemployment rose to 7.1% (LFS 2024-25). Young graduates face high unemployment, especially young women, so a degree alone is not a job guarantee. Skills-linked fields are safer bets.

### Cited Findings
- IT exports were about $4.6bn in FY26, up about 21% from $3.8bn. The government target was $5bn. Uraan Pakistan targets $10bn by FY29 — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2647548/pakistan); [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40431885)
- Freelancer earnings were a record $1.76bn in FY26 (+78%) per ProPakistani citing SBP, while other reports give about $1bn (+50%). These figures conflict. There was a record $197m month in September 2026. There are 2.37 million registered freelancers — [ProPakistani FY26](https://propakistani.pk/2026/07/20/pakistani-freelancers-earn-record-1-76-billion-in-fy26/); [ProPakistani Oct 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/18/freelancers-set-new-monthly-earnings-record/amp/); [meatechwatch](https://meatechwatch.com/?p=47532)
- The FY27 budget extended the preferential tax regime for IT and freelancers by three years — [voj.news](https://www.voj.news/it-exports-pakistan-sbp-freelancer-may-2026/)
- LFS 2024-25 put unemployment at 7.1%, up from 6.3% in 2020-21. It uses new ILO 19th ICLS definitions — [PBS LFS 2024-25 Annual Report](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/LFS-2024-25-Annual-Report.pdf); [The News](https://www.thenews.pk/print/1382960-unemployment-rate-up-from-6-3pc-in-2020-21-to-7-1pc-in-2024-25)
- Degree-holder unemployment was about 10.9%, down from 16.3%, per Geo. Gallup's analysis instead reports graduate unemployment of 18.3%, 32.8% for graduates aged 20-24, and 42.3% for female graduates aged 20-24 versus 26.1% for male. Gallup's baseline differs from the official one — [Geo](https://www.geo.tv/latest/635603-pakistans-unemployment-rate-rises-to-71-in-last-five-years-survey); [Gallup Pakistan](https://galluppakistandigitalanalytics.com/2026/07/24/in-pakistan-a-university-degree-can-mean-a-longer-wait-for-a-job-not-a-faster-one/)
- PBS reports that 1 in 4 highly educated women are unemployed — [The News](https://www.thenews.pk/print/1401714-pbs-survey-says-one-out-of-four-highly-educated-women-unemployed)
- A youth skills gap costs Pakistan about $31.5bn a year — [TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/pakistan-loses-31-5-billion-annually-due-to-youth-skills-gap/)

### Inferences
- Course suggestions should weight IT/CS, software and digital skills (freelance-compatible) as growth fields.
- Recommendations should include practical or skill-based routes (DAE, allied health) and not only degrees.
- The underemployment risk of oversupplied general degrees should be shown honestly. MDCAT seat scarcity also implies many surplus Pre-Medical students.

### Gaps
- No field-wise graduate unemployment breakdown (e.g., engineering vs. medicine vs. arts) from PBS was found.
- Healthcare workforce demand data (nurses, allied health, doctor emigration) was not researched.
- No PIDE report on field-level returns was retrieved.

## Role of parents and counsellors; presenting recommendations

### Takeaway
Pakistani evidence shows that parents and society dominate stream choice and that formal school counselling is scarce. Pakistan-specific best-practice guidance on presenting results to families was not found. The app should treat parents as co-decision-makers and present several reasoned options rather than one verdict.

### Cited Findings
- Counselling is scarce, and authors call for counsellors in schools and colleges — [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1597563); [Tribune letter](https://tribune.com.pk/letter/2066123/student-counseling)
- Students who had a counsellor available or attended seminars had more knowledge of counselling — [IJCMPH](https://www.ijcmph.com/index.php/ijcmph/article/download/1435/1243/5980)
- One Pakistani study found that social and peer factors outweighed family factors in students' retrospective career choice. This complicates the "parents decide" narrative — summarized in search results; original not verified ([search context via INTED/related](https://library.iated.org/view/ZAFAR2011ACA))
- General (non-Pakistan) counsellor guidance discusses managing the tension between student and parent career choices — [Times Higher Education Counsellor](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/counsellor/managing-stakeholders/whose-career-choice-it-students-or-parents)

### Inferences
**Presentation design for the app**
- Show the top 3 stream fits, each with:
  - why it fits (trait evidence),
  - the doors it opens and closes,
  - entry thresholds (60% PMDC/PEC, the MDCAT ratio),
  - a Plan B.
- Include a parent-facing view in Urdu/English with the realistic odds for medicine (about 140k candidates for about 22k seats) and the earnings and growth evidence for IT and other fields.
- Prompt a conversation with a teacher or counsellor before the student commits.

**Language and framing**
- Avoid deterministic labels; use "best fit" and "also strong".

### Gaps
- No Pakistani empirical study was found on how families respond to aptitude-based recommendations.
- No data on the number of school counsellors in Pakistan or provincial counselling policies.
