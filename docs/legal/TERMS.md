# Rahyaab terms of use (draft for review)

**Draft v0.1, 5 Nov 2026.** Must be reviewed before publishing, with an Urdu version.

1. **What Rahyaab is.** A free guidance game that suggests study paths. **It is a starting point for a conversation with your family and teachers, not a decision or a guarantee.** Admission rules change; always check with your board, college or university.
2. **Who can use it.** Students aged 13 and older in class 8–12, or older learners, with parent or guardian consent if under 18. Parents, teachers and school staff use it as described in the privacy policy.
3. **Your account.** Keep your PIN and links private. Staff must keep their password and two-step code secret and must not share accounts.
4. **Fair use.** Don't try to break into, overload, copy or reverse-engineer the service, access other people's data, or upload anything harmful. We may suspend access that puts others at risk.
5. **Staff duties.** Teachers and counsellors use student information only for guiding their own students. They must not decide anything about a student from the app alone, and must not export data except as allowed.
6. **Content.** The game, art, mascot and text belong to the Rahyaab team. Career data comes from the O*NET database (U.S. Department of Labor), used under its Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence with attribution; the terms of any O*NET web service we call are checked before launch (COMPLIANCE.md).
7. **What we can promise.** Rahyaab is a free educational project. We work to keep its information accurate and the service available, and we fix mistakes we are told about, but we can't promise it will always be available or error-free. Its suggestions are a starting point for a family conversation, not professional advice. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under Pakistani law. (Draft: to be reviewed by the university's legal office.)
8. **Changes and ending.** We may change or end the service. Before ending it, we will let you download your data, then delete it.
9. **Contact.** Through your school, or the contact addresses in the privacy policy.
