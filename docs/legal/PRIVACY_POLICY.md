# Rahyaab privacy policy (draft for review)

**Draft v0.1, 5 Nov 2026.** For the pilot. It must be reviewed by the supervisor and the university's ethics or legal office before it is published. A plain-language Urdu version (in the app and at `/ur/privacy`) is required. This draft reflects `docs/security/DATA_GOVERNANCE.md` and `DPIA.md`; if they change, change this too.

---

## Short version (shown in the app)

- We help you choose a study path by looking at how you play our game.
- **We don't ask for your name, phone number, email or photo.** You use a nickname.
- **Nothing leaves your phone until your parent or guardian agrees.** If you are 18 or older, you agree for yourself.
- Your teacher sees your progress and results only if your family agreed.
- Your family sees your report only if **you** send them a link. You can stop sharing at any time.
- **No ads. We never sell your data.** Only a few service providers help us run the service, and none of them gets your game data (§4).
- You can download or delete your data at any time (You → Download or delete my data).

## 1. Who we are

Rahyaab is a Final Year Project at Riphah International University, built by Zahid Bashir, Hamza Butt and Daniyal Sarwar, supervised by Zeeshan Ali. Contact: `privacy@` and `security@` addresses (to be set up before launch). You can also ask your school.

## 2. What we collect

| What | Why | Optional? |
|---|---|---|
| Nickname, class, board, current group, district, age band, "my situation" | To show the right paths and rules | Nickname and class are required |
| Your choices and task results in the game | To estimate your interests and strengths | Required for a result |
| Phone type (low, mid or high memory), Android version, an installation ID made by the app | To keep tasks fair across phones, and to keep your account tied to your phone | Required |
| Marks | To check eligibility more accurately | Optional |
| Your parent's phone number (only if you join without a school) | To send the consent code | Individual path only |
| Gender and school medium | Only for research on fairness; **never** used to choose your path | Optional, only if you join the research |

**We do not collect:** your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts, location, identity numbers (CNIC or B-form) or advertising ID.

## 3. How we use it

- To give you your results, reasons and Plan B.
- To show your teacher (with your family's consent) your progress and any flags, such as "hasn't started yet".
- To show your family your report, if you share it.
- **Research (only if you and your family opt in):** to check that the game measures interests accurately and fairly. Research data is **de-identified**: it has no nickname, uses a separate research ID, puts ages in bands and hides small groups. That makes it very hard to link back to you, but not impossible, so only the research team can see it and every export is logged.

We never use your data for ads, never sell it, and never let an AI service outside our own servers see it.

## 4. Who can see it

| Who | What |
|---|---|
| You | Everything about you |
| Your family | Only the report you send them a link to, until it expires (30 days) or you stop sharing |
| Your teacher or counsellor | Progress, top paths and flags, for consented students in their own class only |
| The Rahyaab team | Only to run and fix the service; every access is logged |
| Service providers | Our hosting company (stores encrypted data); an SMS company (only if a parent gives consent by SMS: the parent's number and a one-time code); an email company (staff email addresses only). They may use the data only to provide that service |

## 5. Where it is stored, and for how long

- **On your phone:** encrypted.
- **On our server:** encrypted, on a server the team controls.
- **How long:**
  - game events: up to 12 months;
  - your profile and results: while you use the app, and then 12 months;
  - **deleted within 30 days if you ask;**
  - a short record that consent was given or withdrawn (no game data): until the study ends plus 1 year.
- Backups are encrypted and expire within 90 days.

## 6. Your rights

- See and **download** your data (You → Download my data).
- **Delete** your data (You → Delete my data), or ask a parent or your school.
- Correct your profile, or update your class.
- Withdraw consent (for parents: on the consent page), or leave the research at any time without losing anything.
- Complain to your school or to us. We reply within 7 days.

## 7. Children

Rahyaab is for students aged 13 and older. The app asks for birth month and year at the start, with no answer pre-selected; under 13, it stops and stores nothing. Students under 18 need a parent's or guardian's consent before any data is uploaded. If we learn that a child under 13 is using it anyway, we delete their data within 30 days, and from backups when they expire (within 90 days). Adults (18+) give their own consent and can use the app, but they are left out of research unless the ethics approval covers adults.

## 8. Security

- encryption on the phone, in transit and on the server;
- two-step login for staff;
- strict access rules;
- regular security testing.

If a breach affects you, we will tell your school and family within 72 hours.

## 9. Changes

When this policy changes, we'll show what changed in the app before it takes effect.
