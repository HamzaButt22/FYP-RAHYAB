# Rahyaab: guide for coordinating teachers

**Draft v0.1, 16 Nov 2026**, for the Riphah ethics application (item 17 in `README.md`). Owner: Hamza Butt. Not yet reviewed by the supervisor. Plain English, about Grade 8 level. Text in `[brackets]` is filled in before printing. The teacher dashboard is still being built, so this guide describes the steps and not the screens.

## 1. What Rahyaab is, and what it is not

Rahyaab is a phone game for students in classes 8 to 10. A student lives a story "year" in four short weeks. From their choices and small puzzles, the app suggests three study paths, with reasons and a Plan B.

- It is **a starting point for a talk with the family.** It is not a test, and there are no marks, timers or "wrong" answers.
- It is **not a decision.** Please never decide a student's group, or tell a family what to choose, from the app alone. Use it with what you know about the student.
- It does not replace a counsellor. The app can be wrong.
- The app is in English only. Students who find English hard can use the read-aloud button. You may explain the screens in Urdu or any language your class uses.

## 2. The consent drive, step by step

No student's data leaves their phone until you record a signed form. The app works before that, but nothing is sent or used.

1. **Check ages first.** Rahyaab is for students aged 13 to 17. Students under 13 cannot take part. Use the admission register, not what the student says.
2. **Send Form A home on paper.** The parent chooses English or Urdu. Give families **at least 5 school days** to decide.
3. **Do not chase families for the research part.** Part 1 lets the child use the app. Part 2 is optional research. A family that says no, or does not answer, is not asked again. No grade, reward or privilege depends on any of it. Do not tell other students who did or did not take part.
4. **Record each returned form.** In the dashboard, open your class, then **Consent forms**, and press **Record** next to the form's number. Copy the child's **age band from the admission register** (13–15 or 16–17). Never copy it from the form or from the student. If the register and the form disagree, the register wins. Also record whether the **teacher box** and the research box are ticked: a student whose form has no tick in the teacher box never appears on your dashboard (their family did not agree to it).
5. **Give the child the tear-off slip.** It has a form number and a one-time claim code. The child types the code in the app. Each code works once. If a child loses it, tell the team: do not write codes down anywhere else.
6. **Form C is only for students who are 18 or over.** Record it only after the register confirms the age. A student who says they are 18 is not enough.
7. **Keep the signed paper forms locked at school.** Do not send them, or photos of them, to the team.

If a parent wants to withdraw, take their request on the withdrawal form or tell the team. Their child's data is deleted within 30 days.

## 3. What you can and cannot see

| You can see | You cannot see |
|---|---|
| Students in your own classes whose families agreed that teachers may see their progress | Students in other classes, or students whose families did not agree |
| Nickname, progress by week, top paths, and simple flags such as "not started yet" | Real names (the app does not hold them) |
| Notes you write yourself (staff only) | A student's marks (optional for students, and not shown to teachers by default) |
| Class totals (groups smaller than 5 are hidden) | Research data, gender or medium records, or any other school's data |

Notes and flags are deleted at the end of the school year. Everything you look at is logged. Please look only when you have a reason.

## 4. What to say to students and parents

**"Is this a test?"**
No. There are no right or wrong answers, no timer and no marks. It is a game that helps you think about your options.

**"Will this decide my child's group?"**
No. It gives three ideas and a Plan B, with reasons. It is a starting point for a talk at home. You and your child decide, and the school still gives its usual advice.

**"Who sees the data?"**
Your child uses a nickname, not their name. You see the report only if your child sends it. Teachers see progress and top paths if you agreed. The research team sees data without names. Nothing is sold, and no adverts are shown.

**"How do we withdraw?"**
Tell the teacher or the team at any time, use the link in the family report, or fill in the withdrawal form. Saying no or stopping does not affect the child at school. Data is deleted from the live system within 30 days.

**"My child did not join the research. Can they still play?"**
Yes. The research part is separate. Every student with a recorded Part 1 consent can use the app.

If you do not know an answer, say so and ask the team: `[Team contact]`. Do not guess.

## 5. Supervised sessions

Some sessions (the research survey, and the usability rounds) are run by the team with you in the room. Please:

- provide a quiet room and `[the time agreed with the school]`;
- stay in the room, as an adult the students know;
- remind students that they can stop at any time without giving a reason;
- never make a student take part, and never ask what a student answered.

## 6. If a student is upset or tells you something serious

The app has no chat or free text, so this can only happen in person. Follow `PROTOCOL.md` §9 and your school's own procedure.

1. **If a student is upset:** stop, offer a break, and say that stopping is fine. Do not carry on with that student that day.
2. **If a student tells you about harm or risk of harm** (to themselves, or from others): listen. **Do not promise to keep it secret.** Do not investigate or ask probing questions. Pass it **the same day** to the school's safeguarding contact: `[Name and phone, from Form D]`.
3. **Write down only what was said and who you told.** Keep that note away from anything about the app, and give it to the safeguarding contact.
4. Tell the team that an event happened, without the student's name: `[Team contact]`. The team reports to the supervisor within 24 hours.

## 7. Never send names or photos to the team

Messages and emails to the team must **not** contain student names, parent names, phone numbers, photos, screenshots showing a student's nickname with their class, or pictures of signed forms. Use the form number, the class and a short description instead ("Form no. 0123, class 9B, a lost slip").
