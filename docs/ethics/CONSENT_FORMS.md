## Form A. Parent or guardian information sheet and consent (school path)

**Title:** Rahyaab: a game that helps students think about which study path fits them.

**Why are we asking?** Your child's school is helping us test Rahyaab, a phone game made by students at Riphah International University. In the game, your child lives a story "year". From their choices and small puzzles, the game suggests 3 study paths (for example a Matric group, an FSc stream or a university field), with reasons and a Plan B. **It does not make decisions for your child.** It is meant to help your family talk about the choice.

**What will your child do?**
- Play about 5–10 minutes at a time, 4 short "weeks" in total, at school or at home.
- **If they also join the research** (optional, Part 2): fill in one short interest survey on paper or a tablet in a supervised session at school (about 15 minutes), and possibly play again after 2 weeks.

**What do we collect?**
- A nickname (**not** your child's real name).
- Class, board, group and district.
- An age band.
- Their game choices and puzzle results.
- The phone type (low, mid or high memory) and Android version, to keep tasks fair across phones. Not the phone's model name or any hardware ID.

**We do not collect** your child's name, phone number, email, photo, location or CNIC/B-form number.

**Who sees it?**
- Your child.
- You, if your child sends you a link.
- Your child's class teacher or counsellor, who sees: progress, top paths, and simple flags such as "hasn't started yet" or "asked for help". Teachers may write short private notes for follow-up; notes and flags are deleted at the end of the school year.
- The research team, without names.

**No ads and no selling.** No other company receives your child's game data. (Only on the individual path, without a school, an SMS company delivers a one-time code to a parent's phone.)

**Risks and benefits.** The main risk is that a suggestion might worry your child or the family. Every result shows a Plan B and says it is "a starting point", and teachers are told not to decide from the app alone. Possible benefits: your child learns about paths and careers, and your family has a clear report to discuss.

**Your choices:**
- Taking part is **voluntary**. Saying no will not affect your child at school.
- You can withdraw at any time. Contact the school or the team, or use the link in the family report. Your child's data is then deleted from our live systems within 30 days, and from encrypted backups when they expire (within 90 days).
- We keep a short record that you gave and withdrew consent (no game data) until the study ends plus 1 year, or for the period the ethics approval sets, as proof that we asked.
- **Research data:** if you withdraw, your child's data is removed from any future research export. Data already included in a de-identified research export before you withdrew cannot be picked out again, because it no longer carries your child's ID.

**Contacts:**
- Team: `[Team contact]`.
- Supervisor: `[Supervisor contact]`.
- Ethics committee: `[Ethics ref no.]`.

## Form B. Student assent (shown in the app as S-04, and on paper)

**Read aloud if needed.**

- Rahyaab is a game about a year in your life. It helps you think about which study path might fit you.
- There are **no right or wrong answers** and **no timer**.
- You use a **nickname**. We don't ask for your name, phone number or photo.
- Your family sees your report **only if you send it**. Your teacher sees your progress if your family agreed.
- The result is **a starting point, not a decision**. Talk about it with your family.
- You can stop at any time, and ask us to delete your data.

**Quick check before the box unlocks** (two taps, no score kept; a wrong tap just shows the right answer):
1. "Can my teacher see my nickname and top paths?" (Yes, if my family agreed.)
2. "Is my result a final decision?" (No, it's a starting point.)

☐ **I understand and want to play.**  ☐ I'd also like to help the research (optional).

## Form C. Adult learner consent (18+, paper, school path)

The same content as Form B, in adult wording. It is printed with a serial number and a tear-off claim code, like Form A, and signed by the student. **The teacher records it only after checking in the admission register that the student is 18 or over**, and copies the band (18–19, 20–24 or 25+). A student's own statement of age is never enough (`docs/security/AGE_ASSURANCE.md`).

☐ I am 18 or older and agree to use Rahyaab as described in the privacy policy.
☐ (Optional) I agree that my de-identified data may be used for research. This box appears only if the ethics approval covers adults; otherwise adults use the app but are never included in research.
